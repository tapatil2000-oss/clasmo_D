Common questions

How do I pay?

After you send your cart, our team calls to confirm tests, rates and the time slot. Payment is arranged on that call.

Do I need to fast before the test?

Some tests such as fasting sugar and lipid profile need an 8–12 hour fast. Each test card shows this, and we confirm again on the call.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Satana, Maharashtra. Call +91 92721 73712 to check home collection in your area.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

Not for routine tests and health packages. Some special tests may need one. You can also send your prescription on WhatsApp and we will pick the right tests.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us before the visit and we will change your time slot.

When will I get my report?

Many routine reports are ready within the same day. Timing depends on the test and we confirm it when you book.
